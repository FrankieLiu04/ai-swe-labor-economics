# Source Inventory

This repository does **not** mirror copyrighted source PDFs/articles. It records source links, research notes, and the role each source plays in the argument.

## Core AI capability and economics

### METR — Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Tasks
https://metr.org/blog/2025-03-19-measuring-ai-ability-to-complete-long-tasks/

Role:
- introduces/updates the task time-horizon framework;
- motivates separating capability from economic viability.

### METR — Expenditure Horizon
https://metr.org/blog/2026-07-21-expenditure-horizon/

Role:
- central inspiration for the cost-performance crossing framework;
- NanoGPT optimization case study;
- human-cost assumptions and sensitivity;
- diminishing returns / agent plateau;
- experiment-compute share and harness concerns.

### METR — Time Horizon Limitations
https://metr.org/notes/2026-01-22-time-horizon-limitations/

Role:
- cautions against translating benchmark task horizons directly into real-world automation;
- motivates reliability and task-risk constraints.

## Labor-market evidence

### Stanford Digital Economy Lab — Canaries in the Coal Mine
https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publication/canaries-in-the-coal-mine-six-facts-about-the-recent-employment-effects-of-artificial-intelligence/

Role:
- young-worker employment/hiring patterns in AI-exposed occupations;
- evidence relevant to flow-before-stock hypothesis.

### Indeed Hiring Lab — The Labor Market Is Tilting Toward Seniority
https://www.hiringlab.org/2026/07/23/the-labor-market-is-tilting-toward-seniority/

Role:
- entry/mid/senior posting composition;
- software-development seniority structure.

### SignalFire — State of Tech Talent 2026
https://www.signalfire.com/blog/signalfire-state-of-talent-report-2026

Role:
- big-tech/startup hiring composition;
- new-grad hiring decline and senior-heavy engineering structure.

### New York Fed — Do Job Postings Show Early Labor Market Effects of AI?
https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/05/do-job-postings-show-early-labor-market-effects-of-ai/

Role:
- important counterevidence / identification warning;
- cautions against attributing all junior hiring weakness to post-ChatGPT AI.

### New York Fed — Remote Work Leaves Younger Workers Sidelined
https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/06/remote-work-leaves-younger-workers-sidelined/

Role:
- alternative causal channel for weak young-graduate hiring;
- reinforces need for remote-work controls.

### Handshake — Class of 2026 / Computer Science
https://joinhandshake.com/blog/network-trends/class-of-2026-spotlight-computer-science

Role:
- college/new-grad market and CS supply-demand context.

### NACE — Demand for AI Skills in Entry-Level Jobs
https://www.naceweb.org/job-market/trends-and-predictions/demand-for-ai-skills-in-entry-level-jobs-nearly-triples-since-fall-2025

Role:
- evidence that entry-level job requirements are shifting toward AI-using capability.
